Starting a Nonprofit in South Carolina

If you are launching a 501(c)(3) organization in South Carolina, your first state-level formation step is usually filing nonprofit formation paperwork with South Carolina Secretary of State – Business Entities. Confirm the current form name, filing fee, registered agent requirements, and annual report rules before submitting anything official.

State Filing OfficeSouth Carolina Secretary of State – Business Entities

IRS StepAfter state formation, apply for an EIN and plan your IRS Form 1023 or 1023-EZ filing if seeking 501(c)(3) recognition.
Ongoing ComplianceAfter formation, most nonprofits should calendar state annual or periodic reports, IRS 990-series filings, and any charitable solicitation registration that applies before fundraising.
